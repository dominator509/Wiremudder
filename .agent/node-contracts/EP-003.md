## Inherited-Source Rule

No inherited path may be edited until exact source evidence exists and `.agent/expected-files/EP-003.discovered.txt` contains the required evidence header and exact path. A broad `src/` authorization is invalid.

## Acceptance Obligations

1. Reference and candidate executions produce normalized semantic traces.
2. Protocol Museum exercises negotiation, latency, disconnect, malformed input, and compression.
3. Fixtures are sanitized and versioned.
4. Comparison distinguishes equivalent, intentional difference, unresolved difference, and reference inconsistency.
5. Replay is deterministic across repeated runs.
6. Implementation-authored tests cannot approve their own new oracle.
