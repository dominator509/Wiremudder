## Acceptance Obligations

1. Threat models cover every trust boundary.
2. No secrets are committed or logged.
3. Dependency, submodule, binary, model, voice, audio, visual, package, and installer provenance is inventoried.
4. Prompt injection and permission denials pass.

6. Critical findings block release.
