## Acceptance Obligations

1. Database choice and versions are pinned by ADR.
2. Gameplay writes are asynchronous and bounded.
3. Transcript append and index recovery are proven.
4. Migrations are backup-aware and resumable.
5. Export, delete, backup, and restore pass live-fire.
6. Secrets remain outside ordinary storage.
