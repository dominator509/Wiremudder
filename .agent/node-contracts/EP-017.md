## Acceptance Obligations

1. Copilot never hidden-sends commands.
2. Suggestions cite observations and memory.
3. Why explains evidence and uncertainty without secrets.
4. Confidence is calibrated and non-authoritative.
5. Context, provider, redaction, token, and cost are visible.
6. Slow or failed AI degrades to no suggestion.
