# License and Source Strategy

WireMudder is designed as an attribution-preserving derivative of Mudlet. EP-000 verifies the exact upstream license files, notices, submodule licenses, package licenses, generated assets, and any exceptions in the pinned repository. EP-002 determines the combined distribution obligations and release notice format.

The safe default is to publish the desktop fork, bridge, WireCore components distributed with it, schemas, build scripts, and corresponding source under compatible open-source terms. Provider services, support, deployment, and independently licensed content may have separate models only after legal review confirms the boundary.

No coding agent may make a binding legal conclusion, remove copyright notices, change the license, or publish an artifact with unresolved license provenance. Such uncertainty is a STOP condition with a recommended conservative default.
